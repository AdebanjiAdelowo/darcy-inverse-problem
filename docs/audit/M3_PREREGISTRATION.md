# Milestone 3 pre-registration

Written 2026-11-16, before the optimiser study and before any MCMC pilot or production chain.
Disclosure: two pilots were run before this was written, on the headline configurations only:
the deterministic smooth and structured reconstruction (N=32, alpha=30) with Euclidean and preconditioned
L-BFGS-B, and the smooth and structured MAP (N=16, r=15) with Euclidean and Gauss-Newton-preconditioned L-BFGS-B.
Their outputs motivated the first-order criteria below. Thresholds were set with margin relative to those
pilot values and are not adjusted afterwards.

All new runs use `OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1`, at most four concurrent single-thread processes,
fixed seeds, and write only under `results/m3/`.

## D. Deterministic optimiser convergence

Configurations: all 46 historical deterministic runs (reconstruction demo 2, regularisation study 9, noise study 5,
sensor study 30), regenerated by the historical scripts with their own seeds and data.

Variants:

- **E-hist**: historical Euclidean L-BFGS-B, historical budget (120; 200 for the demo).
- **P**: regularisation-preconditioned L-BFGS-B (P = alpha K + alpha M), same objective, same SciPy options
  (gtol 1e-6, ftol 1e-12), **same historical budget**.
- **E-2000**: Euclidean control with budget 2000 (diagnostic only: does more budget alone suffice?).

First-order convergence (all three required), evaluated at the returned iterate:

- **C1**: SciPy stopped on its own convergence test (projected gradient or relative function reduction),
  not on an iteration or evaluation cap.
- **C2**: relative reduction of the mesh-independent dual gradient norm, ||g||_{P^-1} / ||g_0||_{P^-1} <= 1e-6.
- **C3**: Gill-Murray-Wright gradient test with the existing tolerance tau_F = ftol = 1e-12:
  ||g||_{P^-1} <= tau_F^{1/3} (1 + |J|).

Also reported, not required: the historical nodal measure ||g||_inf <= 1e-6 (C0), and agreement of mean-free
minimisers between variants. "Demonstrated improvement" = more runs meeting C1-C3 under P than under E-hist,
at the same budget.

## MAP

Variants for both truths at the historical full configuration: historical xi-space L-BFGS-B (120), Euclidean
control (2000), and **P-MAP**: reduced 14-dimensional mean-free KL space with xi_c = 0 (exact MAP of the constant
mode), L-BFGS-B reparameterised by P = I + G^T Gamma^-1 G (Gauss-Newton at xi_R = 0), budget 120.
Criteria C1-C3 as above, with ||.||_{P^-1} in KL coordinates.

## A/B. Constant mode and posterior variance

Sampling target: the exact marginal posterior of xi_R (14 mean-free KL coefficients); pCN kernel unchanged on
that subspace; forward-only likelihood. Constant mode treated three ways from the same chains: analytical
marginalisation, mean-free reporting, independent N(0,1) draws. Required: total pointwise variance >= lambda_c
phi_c^2 = 1 everywhere and integrated variance >= 1; independent draws of xi_c consistent with N(0,1)
(Kolmogorov-Smirnov p > 0.01).

## C. MCMC

Pilot (per truth): single chains of 8,000 proposals from the P-MAP for beta in {0.01, 0.02, 0.04, 0.08}, plus one
chain from a dispersed start. Selection rule: the beta with the largest minimum (over monitored quantities)
bulk ESS per proposal; ties broken toward smaller beta.

Monitored quantities: the 14 coefficients xi_R, the negative log-likelihood Phi, and the mean-free field
m_R at the five historical probe points (0.25,0.25), (0.75,0.25), (0.5,0.5), (0.25,0.75), (0.75,0.75).

Production: 4 chains per truth, seeds 1-4, started at xi_R,MAP + eta with eta ~ N(0, I) (over-dispersed
relative to the posterior), first half of each chain discarded as warmup. Chain length chosen from the pilot so
that the predicted pooled bulk ESS is at least 600 for every monitored quantity (1.5 x the target); the runtime
estimate is recorded before launching.

Convergence target for every monitored quantity: rank-normalised split R-hat < 1.01, bulk ESS >= 400,
tail ESS >= 400. Acceptance rates are not used as convergence evidence. If the target is missed, the result is
reported as not converged, with the diagnostics; no chain is extended or discarded selectively.

## Deviations (recorded before the corresponding runs)

1. **MCMC production length (budget).** The pilot (`results/m3/mcmc/pilot_summary.json`) gave a minimum bulk
   ESS of only 0.17 to 0.45 per 1,000 proposals for every beta, i.e. integrated autocorrelation times of about
   2,000 to 6,000 proposals; the pilot estimates themselves rest on ESS of about 2 and cannot separate the beta
   values reliably. The selection rule picks beta = 0.01 (smooth) and 0.02 (structured), and the length rule
   then requires about 1.05 to 1.2 million proposals per chain, about 5 h per chain at the measured 15-17 ms
   per proposal, i.e. about 10 h of wall time with four processes. That is not run without approval. Instead,
   a **bounded production run** of 4 chains x 100,000 proposals per truth (thin 10, seeds 1-4, dispersed
   starts, same betas) is run now to obtain multi-chain diagnostics, a pooled estimate of the autocorrelation
   time and the corrected variance. Its convergence verdict is reported exactly as measured. The full-length
   run is left PENDING with its exact command.
